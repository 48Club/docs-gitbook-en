# Partnership

## Intro

48Club logo downloads are at the bottom of this page, but please be sure to read these guidelines first.

Although 48Club distributes some of its technology under open-source licenses, the 48Club brand is not distributed under the same licenses.

The following 48Club “Brand Assets” are copyrighted material, and you may only use them in accordance with the policy detailed below:

• The 48Club logo, wordmark, and icon

• The 48Club name

• Other visual assets produced by 48Club, including but not limited to illustrations and 3D models

## What you may do

You may use 48Club’s Brand Assets when you are:

👌 Referring to our products and services

👌 Linking to our official site or products, such as “Explore our services on 48Club”

👌 Announcing an official partnership, but only if you have official approval by a qualified member of the 48Club team

For information on other uses of our Brand Assets, please reach out at info@48.club

## What you may not do

While we strongly believe in and follow the open-source ethos, protecting the 48Club brand is extremely important for our users’ security, so that users know what is a genuine product/endorsement and what isn’t.

Here are a few examples of forbidden uses of our Brand Assets:

⛔️ Don’t use our Brand Assets in your products’ name, logo, NFTs, etc.

For example, making an app called “48Club Engine” or “48Club Junior” is not allowed.

Don’t use the 48Club logo in whole or in part as part of your products’ logos, marketing material, website, or other.

Logos with a strong resemblance to the 48Club icon are also forbidden.

⛔️ Don’t use our Brand Assets to create digital or physical products for sale, including but not limited to:

• Merchandise (“Swag”)

• NFTs

• Other products

⛔️ Don’t create derivative names that imply official endorsement

For example, don’t make a social media group called “48Club Italy” - this sounds official. “48Club Fans of Italy” may be permitted.

⛔️ Don’t change or adapt our Brand Assets in any way

Use the logos and other assets exactly as provided. Do not change the colors, reassemble, abbreviate, or change these assets in any way.

Use your common sense and creativity. If you want to make something relating to the 48Club ecosystem, make it your own.

Have fun making a unique brand that’s clearly yours – if your product is strong, you won’t need to rely on remixing 48Club’s brand.

Let me know if you need further adjustments!
